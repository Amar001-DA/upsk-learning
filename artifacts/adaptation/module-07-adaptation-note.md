\# PM Impact Statement



With the Meridian requirement and the six-day timeline, we can deliver both individual booking and basic company-account booking support for the demo. Users will be able to book services on behalf of employees by providing employee name and email information during booking. To stay within schedule, we are removing advanced search filters, provider analytics, reviews, and non-essential enhancements from the current scope. The highest remaining risk is integration work around delegated bookings and payment-flow validation, but these risks are manageable within the timeline. We will need confirmation from Meridian on how they expect employee booking information to be displayed and managed. Once that confirmation is received, we can proceed with implementation against the updated plan.
